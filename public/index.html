      Valuation

      Each method's value per share, as a range, against today's price. The ranges come from moving each model's main assumption up and down. All models share the discount rate and terminal growth set below.

      DCF & reverse DCF

      Two-stage free-cash-flow model: your growth rate for years 1–5, fading in a straight line to terminal growth by year 10. The reverse DCF asks what growth today's price already assumes.
